Endeavour

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who we are

Endeavour is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what information Endeavour collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using Endeavour you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name provided by that service.
  • Profile: the name, username, bio and profile picture you choose.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, routines, custom exercises, notes, rest times, photos and videos you attach, and settings.
  • Body information you choose to enter: body weight, body fat, height, age and gender. These are used to calculate lift ranks, accolades and calorie targets.
  • Social activity: who you follow, follow requests, leagues, workout tags, likes and comments, people you block, and reports you send about content or other users. Comments are checked automatically for abusive language before they are posted.
  • Food logs and meal presets, if you use the calorie tracker, and photos you choose to scan with the food scanner.
  • Technical data: crash reports and basic device information (model, operating system, app version) to find and fix bugs.
  • Files you import (for example a Hevy or Strong export) are read on your phone and saved as workouts in your account.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save and back up your data, sync it between your devices, and show your training history and statistics.
  • For social features you choose to use: your profile, shared workouts, leagues, likes and comments are shown to the people allowed to see them.
  • To work out ranks, accolades, volume and calorie targets.
  • To send notifications you have switched on, such as the rest timer.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see your information

  • Accounts are private by default: only followers you approve can see your workouts and stats. Anyone signed in can see your name, username and profile picture so friends can find you.
  • If you switch to a public account, anyone signed in can see your shared workouts and stats.
  • Workouts set to Private are never uploaded to the friends feed. Photos on shared workouts are stored at a hard-to-guess web address; anyone who has that address can view the image.
  • Your body weight is only shown on your profile if you turn that on.

Permissions on your phone

  • Endeavour only asks for a permission when you first use the feature that needs it, and you can turn any of them off in your phone’s settings. Each feature keeps working without the others.
  • Camera: to scan food and barcodes, and to take photos for your profile or workouts.
  • Photos: to attach photos and videos you pick to your workouts or profile, or to scan a saved food photo.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only for voice commands in the exercise hold timer, and only while you have the timer’s microphone button switched on (it stops when you leave the app). Your phone listens for the words “start” and “stop”. Endeavour never records, stores or uploads your voice.
  • Notifications: for the rest timer and social updates you switch on.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: only if you turn on App Lock; this stays on your phone and Endeavour never receives it.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and data securely in the cloud.
  • Sentry receives crash reports to help us fix bugs.
  • Google AdMob shows ads to free users and may use device identifiers to do so. Elite members see no ads.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them, and in-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google; we never see your payment details.
  • Food scanner: photos of meals or nutrition labels you scan are sent to Google Gemini to estimate the nutrition. The app asks for your permission before the first photo is sent, and nothing is sent if you decline. Endeavour does not keep these photos on its servers (unless you save one to a meal preset on your device). Google processes them under its Gemini API terms and may use them to improve its services, so avoid scanning photos with people or personal details in them. Barcodes you scan are looked up in the free Open Food Facts database; only the barcode number is sent.
  • Voice commands: what you say while the timer’s microphone is on is turned into text by your phone’s own speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which may process the audio on their servers under their privacy policies. Endeavour only looks at that text for “start” and “stop”. The spoken countdown is generated on your phone.
  • Each provider only receives what it needs to do its job and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists so your history is there when you come back.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account Details → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, workouts, profile, photos, social activity and other data from our servers.
  • You can export your workouts at any time in Settings → Import & export.

Your rights

You can see and change most of your information in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example Australia, the UK or the EU) you may have further rights under privacy law, and you can complain to your local privacy regulator.

Children

Endeavour is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so people can only see what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and the app lock if you want extra protection.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update it here and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
