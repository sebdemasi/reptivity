Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

Endeavour is a workout tracking app made in Australia by the team behind Endeavour (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Endeavour app and website collect, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Australian Privacy Principles and similar privacy laws elsewhere. Please read it with our Terms of Use.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name that service provides. If you use a password, it is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our database provider; we never see it.
  • Profile: the name, username, bio, profile picture and profile banner (a photo or GIF) you choose.
  • Training data: workouts, sets and set types, routines, custom exercises, notes, rest times, the photos you attach and your settings.
  • Body information, only if you choose to enter it: body weight, body fat, body measurements (such as waist or arm size), height, age and sex. We ask for weight, age and sex after you sign up, but every field is optional. This can be health information under privacy law, so we only collect it with your consent (which you give by entering it), and you can change or remove it at any time.
  • Food: your food logs and meal presets if you use the calorie tracker, and photos you choose to scan with the food scanner.
  • Social activity: who you follow, follow requests, leagues, workout tags, likes and comments, people you block, and reports you send about content or other users.
  • Messages you send us: feedback, star ratings and bug reports sent from the app (with your app version and type of phone), and emails you send us.
  • Referrals: the friend code you entered, who used your code, and any reward you claimed.
  • Subscription status: which Elite plan or free trial you have and when it started or ends. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • Your agreement to our terms: which version you agreed to and when, so there’s a record of it.
  • Fair-play checks: if you confirm a lift that is a much bigger jump than your previous best, a record of that lift is kept for our team to review.
  • Technical data: crash reports, your phone’s model, operating system and app version, when you last opened the app (so our team can see how many people are active), and a notification token (stored with your account) so notifications reach your phone. Our providers may also process your IP address to deliver their service.
  • Files you import (for example a Hevy or Strong export) are read on your phone and saved as workouts in your account.
  • We do not collect your location, your contacts, or any advertising identifier.
  • When you have an account, everything above that is saved in the app, including private workouts, body information and food logs, is backed up to your account in the cloud so it can be restored on another phone. If you use Endeavour without an account, your data stays only on your phone.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save and back up your data, sync it between your devices, and show your history and statistics.
  • For the social features you choose to use: showing your profile, shared workouts, leagues, likes and comments to the people allowed to see them, and suggesting people you may know.
  • To work out ranks, accolades, volume, progression advice and calorie targets.
  • To send the notifications you have switched on, such as the rest timer and social updates, and account emails such as confirming your email address or resetting your password. We do not send marketing emails.
  • To manage Elite subscriptions, free trials and referral rewards.
  • To keep the community safe: filtering abusive language in comments, checking photos before they are shown to others, and reviewing reports and flagged lifts.
  • To answer your messages and support requests, fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To meet our legal obligations.
  • We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or use your information to profile you for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Anyone signed in can see your name, username, bio, profile picture and profile banner, and the lift ranks and accolades you choose to showcase, so friends can find you.
  • Accounts are private by default: only followers you approve see your workouts, week streak and monthly volume (and your body weight, if you turn that on) in the app. If you switch to a public account, anyone signed in can see them.
  • Leagues you join show your training volume to the other members, unless you turn off Appear in Lifting Leagues in Settings → Social.
  • To make the app work, those profile figures (streak, volume totals and when you last trained) are kept on your public profile record, which other signed-in users’ apps can technically read even though the app only shows them as described above.
  • Workouts set to Private are never shared or shown to other users. Photos on shared workouts are stored at a hard-to-guess web address; anyone who has that address can view the image.
  • Your age, measurements, food logs and private workouts are never shown to other users.
  • Endeavour’s developers can see any account’s information, including workouts set to Private, to keep the community safe (for example when something is reported or a lift is flagged) and to help with support requests. Every time they do, it is recorded.

Permissions on your phone

  • Most permissions are only asked for when you first use the feature that needs it. On Android, notifications and running in the background are asked for when you first open the app, because the rest timer needs them. You can turn any permission off in your phone’s settings, and the rest of the app keeps working without it.
  • Camera: to scan food and barcodes, and to take photos for your custom exercises.
  • Photos: to attach photos you pick to your workouts, profile or meals, or to scan a saved food photo. Endeavour uses your phone’s own photo picker, so it only ever gets the photos you pick, never the rest of your library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only for voice commands in the exercise hold timer, and only while you have the timer’s microphone button switched on (it stops when you leave the app). Your phone listens for the words “start” and “stop”. Endeavour never records, stores or uploads your voice.
  • Notifications: for the rest timer and social updates you switch on.
  • Running in the background: during a workout the app keeps running when you’re in another app or your phone is locked, so the workout clock, rest timer and rest-over sound keep going. On Android, a notification shows while the workout is in progress; on iPhone, the app plays a silent sound during rests to stay running.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: only if you turn on App Lock; this stays on your phone and Endeavour never receives it.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account, the backup of your app data, your shared workouts and your photos securely in the cloud, handles sign-in, and sends account emails.
  • Sentry receives crash reports to help us fix bugs.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in when you choose them. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play).
  • RevenueCat manages Elite subscriptions for us: it receives your account ID, what you bought and when, and basic device information, so the app knows whether you have Elite.
  • Expo’s push notification service delivers notifications to your phone (for example when someone follows you); it receives your phone’s notification token and the notification itself.
  • Food scanner: photos of meals or nutrition labels you scan are sent to Google Gemini to estimate the nutrition. The app asks for your permission before the first photo is sent, and nothing is sent if you decline. Endeavour does not keep these photos on its servers (unless you save one to a meal preset on your phone). Google processes them under its Gemini API terms and may use them to improve its services, so avoid scanning photos with people or personal details in them. Barcodes you scan are looked up in the free Open Food Facts database; only the barcode number is sent.
  • Photo safety check: when you choose a profile picture, profile banner or workout photo, a smaller copy is sent to Google Gemini to check it doesn’t contain nudity, violence or other content our guidelines don’t allow. The result is only used to accept or refuse the photo; Endeavour doesn’t keep the copy sent for checking. Google processes it under its Gemini API terms.
  • Voice commands: what you say while the timer’s microphone is on is turned into text by your phone’s own speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), which may process the audio on their servers under their privacy policies. Endeavour only looks at that text for “start” and “stop”. The spoken countdown is made on your phone.
  • Each provider only receives what it needs to do its job and handles it under its own privacy policy and security terms. Some of them store or process information outside Australia, for example in the United States or the European Union.
  • Endeavour has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We may also share information if the law requires it (for example a court order), to protect someone’s safety, or with whoever takes over Endeavour if it is ever sold or transferred, who must then protect it in line with this policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists, so your history is there when you come back.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account Details → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, the backup of your app data, workouts, profile, photos, social activity and other data from our database straight away. Copies in our providers’ backups are removed as those backups are replaced, normally within 30 days. If you subscribed, cancel in your App Store or Google Play settings too: deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription.
  • A few records are kept without your account linked to them: feedback messages you sent us, reports other people made (so we can keep the community safe), and a note of any workout our team removed for breaking the rules.
  • Data saved on your phone stays there until you delete the app or a different account signs in on that phone.
  • You can export your workouts at any time in Settings → Import & export.

Your rights

  • You can see and change most of your information in the app, and remove body information or photos whenever you like.
  • You can also ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. We will reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example Australia, the UK or the EU) you may have further rights under privacy law. If you’re not happy with how we handled your information, please tell us first so we can fix it. You can also complain to your privacy regulator; in Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children and teenagers

You must be at least 13 to use Endeavour, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it. If you are under 18, please use Endeavour with a parent or guardian’s permission.

Security

  • Your data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so people can only see what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and the app lock if you want extra protection.
  • If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Our website

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub’s own privacy statement.
