Endeavour · how to delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

Open Endeavour and go to Settings → Account Details → Delete account. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email endeavourgymapp@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Endeavour account” and your username. We’ll confirm it’s your account and delete it within 30 days, and email you when it’s done.

What’s deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • The backup of your app data: workouts (including private ones), routines, body measurements, food logs and settings.
  • Your profile, profile picture, banner and workout photos.
  • Your social activity: posts, likes, comments, follows and league memberships.

Copies in our service providers’ backups are removed as those backups are replaced, normally within 30 days.

What’s kept

A few records are kept without your account linked to them: feedback messages you sent us, reports other people made (so we can keep the community safe), and a note of any workout our team removed for breaking the rules. Data saved on your own phone stays there until you delete the app.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel an Elite subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings so you aren’t charged again.
